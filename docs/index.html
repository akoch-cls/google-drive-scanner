    Scanning tips
    
        
        
      
    
      
    
        
      
          
    1. Hold the page up to the camera by its sides, flat, with all four corners in view. A plain background behind it helps.
      2. 
          
    2. On a Mac, turn off Center Stage (green camera icon in the menu bar, or Control Center → Video Effects). Otherwise the camera follows your face and the page never holds still.
      3. 
          
    3. Watch the outline. It turns green when the page is found, steady and in focus. With Auto-capture on, the page is captured for you.
      4. 
          
    4. Show the next page and it captures again. Each page is straightened and cleaned up automatically.
      5. 
          
    5. Click a page to fix the corners, rotate it, change its look, reorder or delete it. Drag pages in the list to reorder them.
      6. 
          
    6. Save to a Drive folder as a PDF, or turn it in to a Google Classroom assignment.
      7.
